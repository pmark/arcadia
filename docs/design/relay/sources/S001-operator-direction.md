# S001: Operator direction, 2026-11-02

Verbatim, in the order given.

## The request

> I want to integrate an 80/20 Pareto “highest bang per buck” version of DeepSeek’s “super-agent” concept (https://github.com/DeepSeek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype) into Arcadia. I’ve been wanting something similar to OpenAI’s “Dot” that offers Arcadia operators the ability to interact with a single continuous AI thread with a UX similar to rich text messaging.
>
> DeepSeek’s approach goes further by adding triggered agent reactivity that enables the super-agent to observe group messaging channels (e.g. Slack, Discord, IM in general) in order to take active or even passive action by following conversations and being available for direct addressing as a conversation participant.
>
> While I don’t necessarily want to reinvent the wheel, I do want to better understand the feature in the abstract so that Arcadia can have its own implementation of the most relevant and useful benefits to typical Arcadia operators, who are most interested in designing, building and deploying websites, apps, and media assets for themselves and clients using Arcadia’s powerful project planning, management and production systems.

## The process

> What we need is a process that lets me copy and paste plans, proposals, ideas, etc. arbitrarily with confidence that the ultimate proposal stands up to intense scrutiny so that the resultant outcome is in direct alignment with the project mission and desired outcomes.

> Arcadia Design is meant for this exact purpose. Any gaps must be filled now so that we can make the Arcadia super-agent solution (which still needs a good single-term name).

## The name and the first-time operator

> I love "Relay" as the moniker! It should go without saying that Relay has the capability to be the most well-versed and knowledgable source of understanding and wisdom regarding the Arcadia Way and its practical application. Even a first time Arcadia operator therefore shall be empowered to wield the full power of the Arcadia Way via Relay interaction.
