# Decision 0188: Decide whether and how to register the Momentum Keeper as a governed supervisor-class role.

## Options

- **A. Detection only (no helper)**: Build Layer 1 in code: idle-front detection that emits timeline events for critical-path idle minutes by cause (detection lag, no route, waiting on the operator, no ready work), including the no armed wake case (deferral D2), and runs while production is Active and Inactive. No helper role is registered and nothing stocks the queue, so a blocked critical path is detected and recorded but Rowan or the operator must route it and build the next work. Cost: a bounded code change in its own reviewed pull request, near-zero operator attention (no new pings, events only) and no standing token spend. Stays out: any registered helper, front 2 and front 3 work, operator digests, and any new authority.
- **B. Detection plus a governed helper under Rowan that keeps the queue stocked** (recommended): Build option A's detection, and register the Momentum Keeper as a governed supervisor-class helper under Rowan (not a Lead). Rowan or the operator starts each helper under Decision 0114; nothing starts one automatically. The primary measure is critical-path idle minutes by cause, and side fronts never offset it. The helper may route a blocked critical path to its supervising session with one proposed route, run exact-head independent reviews, and keep a worker-mergeable queue stocked (tests and plain docs, held src/ PRs capped at 2, main checked for an existing fix first). It may open reviewed PRs and post reviews but never merges, presses cards, spends, touches credentials or messages the operator, and a host refusal is reported, never routed around. It hands the supervising session a ranked operator queue and a digest at the operator's chosen Morning and evening times (16:00Z and 04:00Z); real-time pings only for a critical-path block on the operator with a tap ready. Sunset rule: fold into Rowan and Relay once timeline --wait, deferral D2 and the stocked queue keep non-operator critical-path idle near 0 for 7 days; revive above 30 minutes a day. Cost: the detection pull request plus a role registration pull request, two operator digests a day, and a recurring helper token spend per run that Rowan or the operator chooses to start; it targets the avoidable half of about 12 operator touches a day. Stays out, as separate Decisions for the operator and not granted here: a multi-day standing grant, a src/ allowlist for worker merge, and a standing merge permission.
- **C. As first proposed (a Lead-class keeper watching all fronts)**: Register the Momentum Keeper as a Lead-class role watching every front under the side-front invariant that at least one front is always moving, with detection code and a helper as in option B. The Critic found this invariant would have scored 0 idle during the 8-hour cap block, because side fronts kept moving while the critical path waited on one tap, so the role would report healthy momentum through the outage it exists to catch. It also widens a role's standing authority above a supervisor-class helper under Rowan, with a heavier recurring token spend and the most operator review of the three. Stays out: the multi-day standing grant, src/ allowlist and standing merge permission, which remain separate Decisions.

## Rationale

Operator direction 2026-11-07 via Signal 2: a role that makes sure work is performed at all times, retried elsewhere when blocked, looking to the horizon for optimizing momentum. The Momentum Keeper has run as an informal helper since about 15:00Z; three previewed proposals (receipts agentask_819d3e8f9a35191a2b, agentask_16f398189e8c4cd292 and agentask_3ad9ba7ff93f97a18a, requests propose-momentum-keeper-role-20261107, -20261107b and -20261107c) captured its design for consideration and granted nothing. This Decision asks the operator whether to make it a governed role and at what size. The operator chose in Signal 2's chat on 2026-11-07: "Momentum Keeper: I choose option B (detection plus a helper that keeps the queue stocked)". Role as proposed: a supervisor-class helper under the production coordinator (Rowan), not a Lead. Primary measure: critical-path idle minutes by cause (detection lag, no route, waiting on the operator, no ready work). A moving side front never offsets critical-path idle time. The Critic's finding on the first proposal: its invariant of at least one front moving would have scored 0 idle during the 8-hour cap block (06:15Z to 14:50Z on 2026-11-07, about 8.5 hours of critical-path idle waiting on one tap) because side fronts kept moving. Baseline from the last 24 hours: about 12 operator touches, about half avoidable. Fronts: 0 route a blocked critical path (when its owner has recorded no route within 10 minutes, send the supervising session the blocker plus one proposed route; when the block needs the operator, have a reviewed, green, one-tap item ready first); 1 observe the production critical path; 2 exact-head independent reviews of agent-authored open PRs lacking a current review; 3 stock the ready queue, preferring work the worker can merge unattended (tests and plain docs), capping held src/ PRs at 2 and checking main for an existing fix before building. Operator-attention front: classify each operator touch as authority-required or avoidable (a defect to remove); keep one ranked operator queue where each item carries the action, one tap, the exact link, its consequence and the critical-path minutes per hour it unblocks; ping in real time only when the critical path is blocked on the operator and the tap is ready; send everything else in a digest at the operator's chosen "Morning and evening" times (16:00Z and 04:00Z, which are 08:00 and 20:00 Pacific); no progress pings; pre-stage items before the operator's known away windows. Wakes: event waiters first (arcadia timeline --wait when shipped, scripts/wait-for-event.sh, the quiet production watch) and a long heartbeat only as a fallback. Record: one JSON row per check plus timeline events; the prose log is a human summary only. Boundaries in every option: the keeper may read status, Issues, PRs and CI, launch bounded helper subagents in isolated worktrees, open reviewed candidate PRs and post marked Independent review comments under routine delivery authorization, and message its supervising session with evidence. It may not merge, press cards, activate or turn off production, spend, touch credentials, delete, publish, message the operator or the coordinator directly, run two writers on the same files, touch other builders' open PRs or gate files they hold, or treat a relayed approval as authority. A host refusal is reported, never routed around. Separate Decisions for the operator, not granted here: a multi-day standing grant with stop rules, a reviewed low-risk src/ allowlist for worker merge, and a standing merge permission that hosts honour. Sunset (options B and C): fold the role into Rowan and Relay once timeline --wait, deferral D2 in docs/design/session-notifications.md and the stocked queue keep non-operator critical-path idle time near 0 for 7 days; revive it above 30 minutes a day. This Decision changes what a supervisor-class helper under Rowan may do without the operator, so the pull request waits for the operator's word and must not be agent-merged.
