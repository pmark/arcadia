# Decision 0191: May Arcadia's deterministic relay launch successor coordinator sessions (Signal, Rowan) by itself when a coordinator's context crosses the handoff threshold?

## Options

- **A. Yes, pinned: the relay launches successor coordinators without an operator step** (recommended): Arcadia's coordinator relay may launch one successor per registered coordinator role (Rowan, Signal) when the outgoing coordinator's context reaches due (60%) or forced (75%), with the same provider and model as the outgoing coordinator, as a host CLI session under tmux in the ordinary interactive permission mode, never a bypass mode. Limits: at most two launch attempts per renewal and six renewals a day, and the standing order expires 30 days after it is answered unless a later Decision renews it. The same answer accepts the rule changes listed in the design: the relay tick on the launchd timer, the workspace capsule as the handoff file, the unattended interactive launch, and an optional custody comment on the coordination Issue. Nothing launches until change 3 (arcadia coordinator renew, with the delivery check, one retry and the alert path) ships in its own reviewed pull request after probes P1 and P2 pass, each probe under its own operator approval and spend bound; until then the arcadia-handoff skill and today's checkpoints remain the procedure. Consequence: handoffs need no operator step, so a coordinator is renewed overnight and in away windows instead of compacting; each renewal costs one fresh session's start-up tokens (orientation and capsule read); the operator is pinged only when both attempts fail or a pre-launch gate refuses. A successor gains no authority its predecessor did not hold: chat approvals do not cross sessions, and merge, spend, credentials, production START and every other gate are unchanged. Stays out, as separate approvals: project hooks (change 6), provider fallback on launch failure, OpenCode coordinators, launching any non-coordinator helper (Decision 0190 covers only the Momentum Keeper) and any merge permission.
- **B. Yes, but ask first: a one-tap Start successor card while awake, automatic at forced**: The relay measures occupancy and prepares the capsule and launch exactly as in A, but at due (60%) it pings a one-tap Start successor card instead of launching; the operator's tap launches the successor. If the card has not been pressed when the coordinator reaches forced (75%), the relay launches the successor itself. Because that forced launch still runs unattended, B needs the same rule changes as A (timer tick, workspace capsule, unattended interactive launch, optional custody comment), and change 3 still waits for probes P1 and P2. Consequence: one operator tap per handoff while the operator is awake, and renewal is automatic overnight; handoffs are slower, because the coordinator keeps working on a filling context between due and the tap or forced; it costs a card surface to build in addition to the launcher, and the same start-up tokens per renewal as A. Stays out: project hooks (change 6), provider fallback, OpenCode coordinators and any merge permission.
- **C. No launches: the relay measures and alerts only**: The relay timer is accepted only to measure and alert: change 1 (coordinator register and budget, with pings at prepare, due and forced), change 2 (the capsule, note, verify and accept, and the custody record) and change 5 (watcher continuity). The timer and the workspace capsule are accepted; launching a successor is not, and change 3 is not built. Consequence: detection and the handoff capsule improve, and the operator is pinged in time, but someone still starts each successor by hand, so a refused or missed start such as the 17:06Z failure can recur and the coordinator compacts or stalls when nobody acts; no launch start-up tokens are spent automatically. Reaching 24/7 unattended production would need a later Decision on launch authority. Stays out: any automatic launch, the Start successor card and project hooks.

## Rationale

The operator directed on 2026-11-07 that compacting context is strongly discouraged and that coordinators must be transitioned to new sessions automatically and deterministically. Evidence from docs/agent-guidance/coordinator-handoff-design.md (PR #2223, head 96071605e, not merged): Signal 2 passed 60% of its 1M-token window at 05:09Z with no trigger; start_session for Signal 3 was refused over Remote Control at 17:06:53Z; the operator then ran /compact at 17:46Z with 977,536 tokens in context. The design proposes a coordinator relay: a small deterministic loop on the existing 60-second launchd timer beside arcadia production liveness-check that measures each registered coordinator's context occupancy from the host transcript (prepare at 50%, due at 60%, forced at 75%), keeps a workspace state capsule, launches the successor as a host CLI session under tmux, verifies delivery of its first prompt and records the custody transfer. Launching coordinator sessions and adding that timer cross existing rules the design lists in its section Rules this design changes: standard-harness.md (no new orchestration code; handoffs go to checked-in files or Agent Asks), the arcadia-handoff skill (no new timer, hook or session launcher), agent-peer-watch.md (posting to GitHub needs a Decision), the operator-only --interactive launch in src/sessions/index.ts, and the AGENTS.md and Constitution rule that launching a session needs authorization. This Decision is the standing order the design names in its Operator Decision needed section, first captured as proposal Agent Ask agentask_7041c526544170380f (request propose-coordinator-relay-standing-order-20261107, preview only). Decision 0190 A covers only starting and stopping one Momentum Keeper at production Active and Off and leaves coordinator successor launch to this Decision. The relay launch is change 3 of the design and waits for probes P1 and P2 (CLI session with --remote-control under tmux, and the --bg stop semantics), which start real sessions and each need their own operator approval and spend bound. Project hooks (change 6) are persistent configuration and stay a separate approval under every option. A reasonable person could choose differently: the question changes what Arcadia launches without a person, amends several standing rules and carries a recurring start-up token cost per renewal. The operator chose A in the Signal 2 session at about 23:20Z on 2026-11-07; that answer is recorded through arcadia decision approve in a separate commit, not by this Ask. The pull requests carrying this Decision are not agent-merged by the helper that prepared them.
