# Decision 0277: Which kinds of choice may agents make for you after heavy-tier advice, under a watch layer, so that only the rest come to you?

## Options

- **1. Delegate four kinds of choice with watch layer** (recommended): Agents decide for you, after heavy-tier advice: plan order, meaning reorder, split or defer existing Actions, never adding an Action or changing acceptance; carrying out Decisions you answered; restarting local Arcadia services outside a live production run; and a clean stop after a failed run, recorded honestly, instead of in-run hand repair (stop and record only: no repair, restart, reset or retry during a live run; if the stop itself would change what production runs, you are asked). Watch layer: before each act a preview receipt names the class, this Decision and its date, the advice source, how to reverse it and when it takes effect; Signal lists each receipt to you, and one word reverses one; receipts settle once a day as one log record that your digest then shows, and a one-tap reverse follows once built and verified. Tripwire: one reversal by you, an undelivered receipt, an unclear class match or no heavy advice sends that kind back to asking until you answer again. An independent process critic audits the receipts weekly, and once a month one batched menu renews every kind with counts of decisions and reversals. Only a Decision you answer can add or widen a kind; a log entry can only narrow one or record a reversal. 'Keeps or narrows authority' is a test each act must pass, not a claim about this delegation, which widens who decides and is therefore yours. Always yours: spend, credentials, START presses and merges you name (never triaged); authority and approval boundaries, governance definitions and production risk, meaning anything that could change what production admits, merges, presses or runs while Active or touches the gate set (always a menu after advice). Nothing gated is merged, approved or pressed under this. Consequence: these four kinds stop reaching you as questions and reach you as receipts you can reverse; everything else still comes to you as a menu.
- **2. Delegate plan order and splits only, with watch layer**: Agents decide for you, after heavy-tier advice, only plan order: reorder, split or defer existing Actions, never adding an Action or changing acceptance. Watch layer: before each act a preview receipt names the class, this Decision and its date, the advice source, how to reverse it and when it takes effect; Signal lists each receipt to you, and one word reverses one; receipts settle once a day as one log record that your digest then shows, and a one-tap reverse follows once built and verified. Tripwire: one reversal by you, an undelivered receipt, an unclear class match or no heavy advice sends that kind back to asking until you answer again. An independent process critic audits the receipts weekly, and once a month one batched menu renews every kind with counts of decisions and reversals. Only a Decision you answer can add or widen a kind; a log entry can only narrow one or record a reversal. 'Keeps or narrows authority' is a test each act must pass, not a claim about this delegation, which widens who decides and is therefore yours. Always yours: spend, credentials, START presses and merges you name (never triaged); authority and approval boundaries, governance definitions and production risk, meaning anything that could change what production admits, merges, presses or runs while Active or touches the gate set (always a menu after advice). Nothing gated is merged, approved or pressed under this. Consequence: the smallest delegation; restarts and run-failure stops still come to you as menus, and carrying out answered Decisions stays allowed under the Constitution.
- **3. Keep asking everything**: Heavy-tier advice is still gathered first, but every choice comes to you as a menu and nothing is decided for you. Consequence: no delegation; the current number of questions, each with advice attached.

## Rationale

Operator, 2026-11-20 (relayed by Signal 16): 'for granular plan/order choices decide what's best long-term, report the reason, and escalate only very significant consequences (authority, spend, credentials, production risk) with pros/cons'; and 'get advice' / 'heavy agent' means briefing an identified agent on the higher tier at high effort before a decision reaches him. Words in chat are not durable authority for later sessions, so this Decision records which kinds of choice agents may make for him and the watch layer that keeps each kind reversible and visible (the Decision Desk section proposed for docs/agent-guidance/consultation.md, draft ellis-desk-consultation-section-20261121.md, is a projection of this Decision). A heavy-tier process-critic review (relayed by Signal 16, 2026-11-21) found an earlier draft widened authority without a watch layer: the delegation changes what agents are authorized to decide, so under CONSTITUTION.md's Authority section (the agent merge exception excludes PRs that change what agents are authorized to do) and Decision 0273 condition 3, this record needs his merge. Option 1 is recommended: it covers the four kinds he has already answered in chat, each narrowed (plan order without new work; restarts outside a live run; carrying out answered Decisions, which the Constitution already allows), under a receipt-first watch layer with a tripwire, a weekly audit and a monthly renewal. 'More information' is the chat menu's fourth reply, not a Decision option. Advice runs at the heavy tier as a named exception to helper-launch's light start, on existing coding-agent plan allocations (Decision 0202), so it adds no spend.
