## Token-efficient transfer rule

Prefer a natural evidence boundary: source published and independently reviewed,
required checks terminal, all helper/check processes terminal, no active source
writer, and the next stage is adoption or a new approval boundary. Handoff sooner
only if context/usage headroom threatens accurate completion. Do not pay for a
fresh coordinator during a repair that already has useful active context.

Keep one brief under roughly 800 words with this index, exact head and main
cursor, applied approval/expiry, live state and its observation time, remaining
allowances, process handles, owners, blockers and one next verb. Link source
reports, reviews and receipts; do not copy histories or logs or fork full history.
A successor reads mandatory governing records and only the triggered procedure
sections, checks both canonical main listings and revalidates approval/custody.
Unchanged passed checks are reused; only changed source or unresolved evidence
justifies replay. Reuse the existing implementer for a repair delta and the
independent reviewer for delta review; no extra helpers without a named gap.

Prepare the fresh-session prompt at that boundary. A title, draft prompt, idle
flag or ended turn is not a transfer. Use a supported, explicitly authorized
host handoff; record successor identity and verified prompt delivery, then park
this coordinator and transfer sole custody. No timer, automation, predecessor
message or second concurrent coordinator. Until an actual successor is verified,
this chat remains responsible. The Supervisor reads this state table and linked
proofs; notifications occur on material changes, not unchanged waiting.
